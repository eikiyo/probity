The conversion price of the Series C Stock is subject to broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution adjustment in the event the Company issues securities, other than certain excepted issuances and subject to certain limitations, at a price below the then current conversion price.   The Series C Stock is not entitled to a liquidation or dividend preference. Beginning on January 1, 2014, the Series C Stock is entitled to 10% accruing dividends per annum. The dividends are payable quarterly in cash, beginning on March 31, 2014. Beginning on May 31, 2014, the shares of Series C Stock shall be redeemed by the Company upon the request of the holders of at least a majority of the then outstanding Series C Stock, to the extent funds are legally available for such redemption.
