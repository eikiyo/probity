The sales price of the Series B preferred stock remained constant at $1.39 per share in several transactions from January 3, 2001 through February 3, 2003. Each share of the Company's 18 Series B preferred stock is convertible into common stock on a 1-for-1 basis and holds a liquidation preference of $2.89, or two times the original issue price. Given the magnitude of the liquidation preference, the values assigned to common stock awards at this time were deemed to be at fair market value. The Company completed the Series C preferred stock financing in June 2004, with a sale price of $1.89 per shares of Series C preferred stock.
