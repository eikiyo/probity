In addition to customary adjustments of shares and Exercise Price in connection with stock splits, recapitalizations, dividends and the like, the Warrants contain a broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution provision which is triggered by sales of Common Stock or other securities convertible into Common Stock for a price per share that is less than the Exercise Price in effect at such time.
