NeighborhoodFind, based in Charlottesville, Virginia, provides a website that serves as a community resource, neighborhood communications tool and relocation solution. With more than 19,000 cities online, the site includes thousands of U.S. neighborhoods and serves as a one-stop resource for community information. The site offers neighborhood details, photos and communication tools, including Neighborhood News(R), Community Calendar, Local Government News, free websites, local school and business information, and relocation services.
