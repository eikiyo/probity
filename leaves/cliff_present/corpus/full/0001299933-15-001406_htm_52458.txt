On September 18, 2015, the Registrant agreed to issue Ms. Angelina Galiteva, Director of the Registrant, 7,750,000 non-qualified stock options out of its existing stock option plan. 50% of the stock options will vest immediately with the remaining 50% vesting quarterly over two years. The stock options were issued at $0.0041 per share. On September 18, 2015, the Registrant agreed to issue Dr. Kevin Schewe, Chairman and Director of the Registrant, 46,000,000 non-qualified stock options out of its existing stock option plan. 50% of the stock options will vest immediately with the remaining 50% vesting quarterly over two years. The stock options were issued at $0.0041 per share. On September 18, 2015, the Registrant agreed to issue Mr. Khurram Irshad, Director of the Registrant, 3,000,000 non-qualified stock options out of its existing stock option plan. 50% of the stock options will vest immediately with the remaining 50% vesting quarterly over two years. The stock options were issued at $0.0041 per share. On September 18, 2015, the Registrant agreed to issue Dr. Carl Kukkonen, CTO and Director of the Registrant, 35,000,000 non-qualified stock options out of its existing stock option plan. 50% of the stock options will vest immediately with the remaining 50% vesting quarterly over two years. The stock options were issued at $0.0041 per share. On September 18, 2015, the Registrant agreed to issue Mr. Stephen Muzi, CFO of the Registrant, 23,250,000 non-qualified stock options out of its existing stock option plan. 50% of the stock options will vest immediately with the remaining 50% vesting quarterly over two years. The stock options were issued at $0.0041 per share. Additionally, the Registrant and its officers and directors agreed to cancel 80,000,000 stock options that were previously issued to the following directors and officers: 7,750,000 stock options to Ms. Galiteva; 11,000,000 stock options to Dr. Schewe; 3,000,000 stock options to Mr. Irshad; 35,000,000 stock options to Dr. Kukkonen; and 23,250,000 stock options to Mr. Muzi. Additionally, the Registrant, Dr. Kukkonen and Mr. Muzi agreed to modify certain terms of previously issued stock option grants that were issued on July 30, 2012 and June 22, 2015. The strike price of these stock options were not modified, but the type of stock option was modified along with the post-termination exercise period.
