See Section 2.1.4 of the Operating Agreement. These rights, privileges, preferences and restrictions include:   •   Voting rights;     •   Conversion rights;     •   Allocations of profits and losses;     •   Distributions of all kinds, including those payable upon dissolution, liquidation or winding-up; and     •   Redemption rights. The Protective Provisions for the Units and the Preferred Units limit the Board’s power to create Units or series of Preferred Units.
