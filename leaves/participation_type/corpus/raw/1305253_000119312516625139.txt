Dividends : The Series A convertible preferred stock and Series A-1 convertible preferred stock shall be entitled to receive dividends (on a pari passu basis) at a rate per annum of $1.4625 per share and $0.5302 per share (after reverse stock split and exchange ratio), respectively, subject to adjustment for stock splits, combinations, reorganizations and the like, when and if declared by the Board of Directors. After payment of the dividends on the convertible preferred stock, any additional declared dividends shall be distributed between the holders of convertible preferred stock and common stock on a pro-rata as-converted basis. No rights shall accrue to the holders of the convertible preferred stock if dividends are not declared and any dividends declared are non-cumulative. No dividends have been declared or paid through December 31, 2015.
